Mushroom Field Guide

Browse through our card collection below to learn about different types of mushrooms.

Chanterelle

Type: Edible

Easily recognized by:

  1. bright golden-yellow color
  2. funnel shape
  3. pleasant apricot-like aroma.

Fly Agaric

Type: Poisonous / Iconic

Famous for:

  1. classic bright red cap
  2. white spots
  3. highly used in fairy tales and folklore!

Morel

Type: Edible

Known for:

  1. distinct honeycomb-like appearance
  2. prized taste by foragers in the spring
