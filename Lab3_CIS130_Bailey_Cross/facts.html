Mushroom Trivia

Welcome to the learning hub! Mushrooms are stranger and more wonderful than most people realize. Explore the facts below and commit them to memory to impress your friends!

Fun Facts!

1. The Underground Super-Network

Mycelium is a massive underground network of fungal threads that connects plants and trees, allowing them to share nutrients and chemical warning signals—often called the "Wood Wide Web."

2. Closer to Animals than Plants

Genetically speaking, fungi are actually more closely related to animals (including humans) than they are to plants! They breathe in oxygen and exhale carbon dioxide just like we do.

3. Bioluminescent Glow

Some species of mushrooms glow in the dark! There are over 80 known species of fungi that emit a greenish light, usually to attract insects that help spread their spores.

What is the largest living organism on Earth?

It's a fungus! Specifically, a specimen of Armillaria ostoyae (honey mushroom) in Oregon covers over 2,384 acres and is thousands of years old.

Can fungi survive in extreme environments?

Yes! Fungi have been found surviving in extreme radiation inside the Chernobyl nuclear reactor ruins and even thriving in outer space.
